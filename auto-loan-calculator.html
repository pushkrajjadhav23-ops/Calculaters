How this is calculated

The amount you actually finance isn't just the car price — it accounts for your down payment, trade-in credit, and sales tax on top.

Sales tax = (Price − Trade-in) × Tax rate

Amount financed = Price − Down payment − Trade-in + Sales tax

Monthly payment = Amount financed × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]

This follows the common convention where trade-in value reduces the taxable amount — most US states work this way, though it's worth double-checking your state's specific rule.

Worked example

A $35,000 vehicle, $3,000 down, $5,000 trade-in, 7% sales tax, 6.5% APR, over a 60-month term:

ItemAmount
Sales tax (on price minus trade-in)$2,100
Amount financed$29,100
Monthly payment≈ $569
Total interest over 5 years≈ $5,062
Total payment≈ $34,162

The trade-in does double duty here — it lowers both the amount financed and the taxable price, which is why it's worth negotiating trade-in value seriously rather than treating it as an afterthought.
