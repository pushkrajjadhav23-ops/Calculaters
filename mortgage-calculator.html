How this is calculated

Mortgage payments use a fixed-rate amortization formula. Every payment is the same amount, but the split between principal and interest changes each month because interest is charged on the remaining balance.

M = P × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]

M = monthly payment  ·  P = loan amount  ·  r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)  ·  n = number of payments (years × 12)

The Split chart above shows how your total cost divides between principal (teal) and interest (brass). A longer term or higher rate grows that interest slice — a quick visual read on how much of what you pay is the actual cost of borrowing versus what you're building in equity.
