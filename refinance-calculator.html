How this is calculated

We calculate your current and new monthly payments using the standard fixed-rate amortization formula, then compare them.

Monthly savings = Current payment − New payment
Breakeven (months) = Closing costs ÷ Monthly savings

The Split chart above shows your new loan's principal versus interest over its full term — useful context alongside the breakeven number, since a lower payment from a longer term can mean paying more interest overall even after the closing costs pay for themselves.

Worked example

A $300,000 balance at 7.2% with 27 years remaining, refinanced to 6.2% over a new 30-year term, with $6,000 in closing costs:

If you plan to stay in the home longer than about 20 months, this refinance pays for itself. Shorter than that, and you'd move before recovering the closing costs.
